# Điện ảnh (Cinematic)

## Mô tả

Phong cách điện ảnh chuyên nghiệp. Độ sâu trường ảnh nông, máy quay chuyển động có chủ đích, ánh sáng kịch tính. Dành cho trailer, phim thương hiệu và nội dung kể chuyện.

## When to use

- Short films / movie trailers
- Brand films, storytelling
- Premium music videos
- Cinematic narrative content

## Prompt writing guide

1. **Subject** — who/what is in frame? What are they doing?
2. **Setting** — where does it take place? Describe the environment
3. **Movement** — how do the subject and camera move?
4. **Atmosphere** — overall emotional feel of the scene

## Camera & Movement

### Recommended
- **Dolly** — camera glides forward on tracks, creates sense of discovery
- **Tracking** — camera follows the subject, creates action feel
- **Crane Up** — camera rises high, creates epic ending
- **Pan Left/Right** — sweeps across to establish setting

### Avoid
- ❌ **Fixed** — too static, lacks cinematic feel
- ❌ Excessive **Handheld** shake — unless intentionally documentary style

## Lighting

### Recommended
- **Golden Hour** — warm sunrise/sunset light, best for cinematic
- **Dramatic** — strong contrast, creates depth
- **Backlit** — light from behind subject, creates rim glow
- **Rim Light** — edge lighting separates subject from background
