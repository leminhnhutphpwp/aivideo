# Giới thiệu sản phẩm (Product Reveal)

## Mô tả

Trưng bày sản phẩm cao cấp, sạch sẽ. Ánh sáng studio, máy quay xoay quanh mượt mà, nền tối giản. Dành cho ra mắt sản phẩm, demo phần cứng và nội dung unboxing.

## When to use

- New product launches
- Feature demonstrations
- Unboxing / review videos
- E-commerce commercials

## Prompt writing guide

1. **Product name** — be specific about product, color, material
2. **Camera angle** — front/back/side? Close-up detail?
3. **Setting** — dark studio, white background, or real environment?
4. **Highlight** — which feature needs emphasis?

## Camera & Movement

### Recommended
- **Dolly** — orbit arc around the product
- **Zoom In** — push into product detail
- **Fixed** — static angle for technical specs

### Avoid
- ❌ **Handheld** shake — loses premium feel
- ❌ Fast movement — viewers can't see the product

## Lighting

### Recommended
- **Studio Soft** — diffused studio lighting, even
- **Rim Light** — edge glow separates product from dark background
- **Natural** — real environment lighting
