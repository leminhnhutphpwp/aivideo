# Quảng cáo mạng xã hội (Social Ad)

## Mô tả

Nội dung mạng xã hội nhanh, dọc. Màu sắc đậm, cắt cảnh nhanh, thẩm mỹ đang thịnh hành. Dành cho TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts.

## When to use

- TikTok / Reels / Shorts ads
- Viral / trend content
- E-commerce product videos
- Behind-the-scenes, day-in-the-life

## Prompt writing guide

1. **Hook in first 3 seconds** — surprise element, color, movement
2. **Clear subject** — one person, product, or action
3. **High energy** — bright colors, fast movement
4. **Think vertical** — always frame for 9:16

## Camera & Movement

### Recommended
- **Handheld** — authentic feel, trending style
- **Zoom In/Out** — creates rhythm on beat
- **Pan Left/Right** — quick sweep across product

### Avoid
- ❌ Slow, static camera
- ❌ Too cinematic — not suitable for mobile

## Lighting

### Recommended
- **Neon** — colorful, urban vibe, stands out
- **Natural** — everyday content feel
